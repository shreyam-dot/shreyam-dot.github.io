About

Content that's equal parts strategy and story.

I'm a senior content and social media lead who owns the whole function: strategy, production and delivery.

I started in broadcast at Channel [V] India, producing 10+ reality shows. I spent nine years as a freelance video producer for brands like Wipro and Harley Davidson, led content for Nas Summit inside the Nas Daily ecosystem, and ran influencer and content at MobiMatter.

AI is my specialty and my main creative tool for films and content. My AI short The Influencer Index has won awards at SIAFF and NY Short Film Monthly and was selected for the Berlin Sci-Fi Filmfest.

Today I run Sugar Habibti Films, a Dubai studio building social-first storytelling for premium brands and founders. I lead teams, brief creators, read the analytics, and still know my way around an edit timeline.
